## 01 · Structural baseplate

The baseplate is the structural layer beneath the arena. Its purpose is to keep the arena, thermal hardware and surrounding supports in a fixed relationship. The supplied model was made for a wooden plate, but the same geometry can be reproduced in acrylic, aluminium or another sufficiently rigid sheet material.

[Download the baseplate STEP file](../downloads/cad/wooden-baseplate.step)

## 02 · LED matrix octagon and access hinge

Eight 64 × 32 LED panels form the octagonal visual display. Printed legs connect adjacent panels and hold the faces at the required angle. The legs can be printed in TPU or PLA. One panel is mounted on hinges so it can open while flies are loaded, then return to the same position for the experiment.

[Download the LED matrix octagon STEP file](../downloads/cad/led-matrix-octagon.step)

## 03 · Infrared-light stand

The lighting stand uses a PVC pipe as its vertical support. A printed lampshade carries the infrared array, and a diffuser above the array spreads the illumination over the arena. The PVC pipe can be replaced by a printed tube while retaining the same support and lampshade arrangement.

[Download the IR light stand STEP file](../downloads/cad/ir-light-stand.step)

## 04 · Thermal ring

The thermal ring is printed in PLA. Copper wire passes through the internal channel and acts as the resistive heater when connected to its controlled power circuit. Small openings around the ring allow air exchange instead of forming a fully sealed barrier.

[Download the thermal ring STEP file](../downloads/cad/thermal-ring.step)

## 05 · Cool-spot registration mask

The mask is placed over the arena before an experiment so the four target positions are visible in the behaviour camera image. These reference positions are recorded for later analysis. The mask is removed before flies are introduced, leaving the registered image coordinates aligned with the physical Peltier tiles.

[Download the cool-spot mask STEP file](../downloads/cad/cool-spot-mask.step)

## 06 · Peltier tile holder and fan support

The tile holder supports the arena baseplate around the four Peltier targets and keeps the layers mechanically stable. Its lower space locates the fan beneath the assembly. Airflow removes heat from the hot side of the cooling hardware and limits heat accumulation below the arena.

[Download the Peltier tile holder STEP file](../downloads/cad/peltier-tile-holder.step)
