## A related question, a distinct assay

The sociability project is based on the **25 November 2024 version** of *Conspecific sociability is regulated by associative learning circuits* by **Victor Lobato-Rios, Thomas Ka Chung Lam, and Pavan Ramdya**. The source version matters: this page describes the project's chosen reference rather than silently substituting methods from a later publication.

That preprint examines how experience with other flies and their odours changes subsequent social responses, and relates those changes to associative learning circuitry. These are findings from the reference study, not completed results of the present apparatus project.

## Shared technical foundation

The two projects share practical needs: controlled arenas, stable imaging, reproducible illumination, and individual identity through interactions. However, successful navigation to a cool target and a social response to another fly are different behavioural outcomes. A place-learning latency should not be relabelled as a sociability measure.

| Dimension | Visual place learning | Sociability development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main behaviour | Navigation relative to visual landmarks and a cool target | Responses to conspecific encounters |
| Project arena record | 20 cm inner walking diameter | Separate 5 cm Delrin arena under development |
| Reference | Ofstad, Zuker & Reiser (2011) | Lobato-Rios, Lam & Ramdya (2024 version) |
| Core measurement issue | Target latency and search trajectory | Reliable identity and interaction-related behaviour |
| Current site status | Apparatus, protocol, analysis, preliminary summaries | Reference-led design direction; no project outcome dataset released |

## Current engineering questions

The small arena is intended to sit uniformly on acrylic. Attachment must not create uneven placement or uncontrolled residues and odour cues. Panel access and illumination support are also being refined. These are practical development questions; no particular adhesive, surface treatment, or fixture is certified as behaviourally neutral here.

## Next evidence to assemble

Extract a dedicated assay protocol from the selected paper, including animal housing and handling, arena conditions, encounter definitions, and data processing. Validate image quality and identity through close contacts in this arena geometry. Only then define an analysis package whose social metrics correspond to the reference study.

Keep the sociability dataset and configuration distinct from the place-learning dataset, even if the same camera or tracking software is used.
