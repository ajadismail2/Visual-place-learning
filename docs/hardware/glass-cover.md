title: Glass cover
description: Fit and handling of the glass cover above the walking arena.

---
The glass cover closes the arena above the thermal ring while leaving the walking surface visible to the overhead camera.

## Fit

Seat the glass cover on the assembled ring without pressing on the temperature sensor or its wiring. Check clearance around the full perimeter and confirm that the glass cover can be removed and replaced without shifting the arena.

## Before recording

Clean both faces and inspect the camera view for reflections, condensation and obscured edges. Keep the glass cover in the same position across recordings.
