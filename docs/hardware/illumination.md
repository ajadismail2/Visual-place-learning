---
title: Illumination
description: Infrared lighting and supports for behaviour recording.
section: Hardware
---
Four infrared LED arrays illuminate the arena for the behaviour camera. Each array sits in a printed shade with a diffuser, mounted on a PVC stand.

Arrange the lights for even coverage and angle them to reduce reflections from the glass cover. Use the arrays' rated supply voltage and keep the stands clear of the arena.

Before recording, check that flies remain visible at the centre and edges without glare or motion blur. Keep camera exposure and gain fixed.
