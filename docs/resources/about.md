## Purpose

This project develops a cost-conscious visual place-learning apparatus for *Drosophila melanogaster*, together with the computational tools needed to inspect behaviour at the level of individual animals. It brings together thermal engineering, visual stimulation, mechanical fabrication, imaging, and quantitative analysis.

The project is developed and documented by **Ajad Ismail**. The site is intended for researchers reviewing the scientific rationale, colleagues evaluating the apparatus, and builders seeking a transparent record of design decisions.

## Working principles

**Accessible construction.** Use widely available components where they can meet the experiment's requirements, and document their limitations.

**Modular development.** Test thermal, visual, mechanical, and imaging systems independently before interpreting integrated behaviour.

**Traceable analysis.** Retain raw observations, calibration, identity mappings, inclusion decisions, and the definitions behind every metric.

**Measured claims.** Distinguish the original study's findings, the local implementation, preliminary observations, and work still in development.

## Research scope

The main platform adapts the visual place-learning paradigm of Ofstad and colleagues. A separate sociability direction draws on the selected Lobato-Rios, Lam, and Ramdya preprint. The website describes their common technical needs without merging the two assays or their expected outcomes.

The current record is an evolving methods and engineering resource. It does not claim a completed, independently replicated biological result or a finalized commercial instrument.

## Contact and contribution

Use the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ajadismail2/Visual-place-learning) to inspect source history. Technical questions, corrections, and reproducibility feedback can be submitted as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ajadismail2/Visual-place-learning/issues).

When reporting a problem, include the relevant hardware/software revision, recording geometry, input/output descriptions, and steps that reproduce it. Remove credentials and unrelated personal information before sharing logs.
