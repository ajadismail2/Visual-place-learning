01 / THE QUESTION

A small arena.
A fundamental problem
in memory.

Can visual landmarks guide a fly back to a safe location when the environment changes?

In a thermal–visual arena, a cool target offers relief from a warm floor. Moving the target together with the surrounding panorama preserves their spatial relationship, allowing visually guided navigation to be tested across trials.

This project adapts the Ofstad, Zuker & Reiser paradigm into a modular, cost-conscious apparatus and a transparent analysis workflow.
